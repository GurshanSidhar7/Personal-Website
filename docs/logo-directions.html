Five quieter directions.

Starting points for discussion. The portfolio currently uses the name-only direction.

01 / RECOMMENDED

The wordmark

The name does the work. No symbol to compete with the typography.

02 / INITIALS

GS ligature

Interlocking serif initials. Personal, compact, and useful at small sizes.

03 / INK

Open-circle G

A nearly complete ink circle, resolved into a G. Quiet and slightly imperfect.

04 / STRUCTURE

Folded G

A continuous folded strip. More geometric, with a subtle origami reference.

05 / ELEMENTAL

Brush flame

One fluid, asymmetrical shape. A restrained fire reference without pixel art or a character.

These are concept directions, not final logo artwork.
